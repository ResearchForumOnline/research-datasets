# Zero Original Pilot 2026-10-09

100 original authored fictional scenarios: 60 train, 20 validation, 20 test across 94 scenario groups. This remains the canceled experiment preparation dataset, not a completed trained model or a restarted training run. Original held-out checks and memorization probes are published separately and remain evaluation-only.

## Rights and provenance

Original dataset records: CC BY 4.0. New standalone dataset generator/validator: MIT. Source methodology references and source-span hashes are attribution/copy checks, not imported source prose.

## Privacy and scientific limits

Only explicitly allowlisted data files were staged. Token/private-key, raw-genotype-row, contact-detail and user-path screens were applied; flagged rows are held rather than rewritten. Pattern screens and bounded source review do not prove absence of every private fact. No private DNA, client/account databases, live logs, credentials, restricted provider outputs or model weights are included.

Synthetic answers may contain errors. These are small datasets, not a trained or validated model. No training, inference or model-quality result was produced by this publication review. Preserve evaluation partitions; never train on held-out answers/probes.
