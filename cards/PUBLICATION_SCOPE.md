# What this release includes and excludes

This release publishes actual reviewed payloads, rather than only descriptions
or placeholder installers. The exact asset list and hashes are in catalog.json.
Cards inside each ZIP explain provenance, schemas, licenses and use limits.

## Included

- Original authored AI curricula, preference/evaluation records and a synthetic
  pilot with generator, validator and scenario-group information.
- Reviewed portions of two historical AI mixtures, with aligned row provenance,
  source-commit licenses and required upstream notices.
- Aggregate reference/model data, genomic marker definitions and corrected
  ancient-source aggregate frequencies for the stated GRCh37 build.
- A separate, exact mirror of seven previously public AADR v14 HO/compatibility-HO
  reference files, including individual-level reference genotypes and their public
  metadata. Filename, size and MD5 match the official unrestricted CC0 release;
  these are third-party scientific references, not private author/client DNA.
- Explicitly synthetic software fixtures where their constructed origin was
  verified; these are not biological validation cohorts.
- Selected categorical and numeric historical software benchmark measurements.
- A separately reviewed MIT-licensed numeric simulation with 150,000 fictional
  agent/environment episodes; it contains no human genotype data.

## Excluded or held

- Personal DNA, customer/client records, account databases, credentials, tokens,
  private keys, private reports, correspondence and private configuration.
- Private individual genotype files and target-derived genotype caches. The
  unchanged official public AADR mirror has its own documented source and scope;
  local additions, participant uploads and private reports are not included.
- Installed virtual environments, operating-system files and runtime/application
  archives. Dependency recipes are metadata, not runnable installed environments.
- Imported training records whose exact source license could not be established,
  records outside this derivative's chosen permissive license scope, and records
  flagged for contact/path or unresolved credential-value review. Whole records
  were omitted from the derivative; possible credentials were never tested.
- Larger legacy corpora not fully cleared by this review. Some have pre-existing
  public upstream releases, linked separately without implying fresh clearance.
- Provider-restricted research or outputs outside the author's redistribution
  authority. Publishing this catalog does not license unreviewed collections.
- Missing optional pack payloads. GRCh38 composition files and several historical
  regional/archaic/population pack candidates were not present in the audited
  source collection. Metadata naming them is not evidence of an uploaded pack.

Private and unreleased original files are preserved. Selected large published
copies are removed locally at the owner's request only after complete public
restore and hash verification; local deletion receipts and recovery commands are
retained. This catalog does not claim every file on the
author's computers has been published or reviewed. Scientific interpretation,
population coverage, consumer calibration and independent model evaluation are
separate from verifying bytes, source attribution and format integrity.
